About Us

Where Fashion Meets Distinction

Corid Lifestyle NG was born from a simple belief — that every Nigerian deserves to look classy without breaking the bank. We started with premium Grade A preowned fashion, bringing luxury wardrobes within reach of everyday people who love quality. But we knew we could do more.

Today, we offer both Grade A Preowned and Brand New collections — giving you the freedom to choose what fits your style and budget. From boardroom sophistication to weekend comfort, every piece we offer is thoughtfully curated to help you step out with confidence — every single day.
